# Yet Another Coding Club (YACC)

**_Coding Club of Indian Institute of Technology Palakkad_**



## About Us

Yet Another Coding Club (YACC) is the official coding club of IIT Palakkad. We are a community of students dedicated to fostering a culture of programming, problem-solving, and software development on campus. Through regular sessions, contests, and collaborative projects, YACC aims to help members strengthen their technical skills and engage meaningfully with the broader programming community.

## What We Do

- **Competitive Programming** : Practice sessions, contests, and preparation for platforms such as Codeforces, CodeChef, and LeetCode.
- **Internship & Placement preparation** : Help students with internship and placement preparation. 
- **Workshops & Bootcamps** : Sessions on data structures, algorithms, web development, game developement, and other technical topics.
- **Open-Source Projects** : Collaborative development of tools and applications maintained under this organization.
- **Hackathons** : Organizing and participating in hackathons and coding events.

## Repositories

This organization hosts the source code for club-maintained projects, workshop materials, contest archives, and internal tools. Please refer to individual repositories for project-specific documentation and contribution guidelines.

## Contributing
